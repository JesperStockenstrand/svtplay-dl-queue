Where to save

Where finished videos are saved. ~ means your home folder. The folder is created if it doesn't exist.

Saves films in one folder and series episodes in another. That's how Plex, Jellyfin and Kodi expect them, with one library for films and one for series. Anything with an episode number counts as a series.

This needs svtplay-dl installed with Homebrew, pip or pipx. With your svtplay-dl, everything is saved in the download folder.

Video

Lower quality gives smaller files. If a video isn't offered in the quality you pick, the download can fail. Then switch back to “Best available” and retry it.

MP4 plays almost everywhere: phones, TVs, QuickTime. MKV handles several subtitle languages better and works well in VLC, Plex, Jellyfin and Kodi.

Subtitles

Subtitles

Get every subtitle language the service offers, not just the default one. Some services also offer a version for the hard of hearing.

Extras

Save the program's cover image next to the video.

Saves an .nfo file with the title, description and episode number. Kodi, Jellyfin and Plex use it to show your downloads with proper names and descriptions.

Adds chapter markers when the service has them, so you can jump between parts of the program in your player.

Whole series

These apply when you choose “All episodes” in the popup or the right-click menu.

Download only the latest episodes, for example the 5 most recent. Leave it at 0 to get every episode.

Also download short clips and extra material, not just full episodes.

Queue

One at a time is the most reliable and leaves bandwidth for everything else. On a fast connection, two or three at once can finish a long queue sooner.

Show a system notification when a download finishes or fails, even when the browser is closed.

Advanced

Leave empty to find it automatically.
